Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eleventh Reset Paradox handles information when you use the app.

Local Game Data

The app stores game progress, chapter completion, choices, evidence, and audio and vibration preferences locally on your device. This information is used to provide the game experience and is not used by us to identify you.

Advertising and Tracking Permission

The app may show advertisements after completed chapters. After the app becomes active, it requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. If you allow tracking, the app may access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) for advertising purposes. If you do not allow tracking, the app continues to function and initializes its security and advertising flow without an IDFA.

Advertising delivery may use third-party advertising SDKs, including Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi. These providers may process device, advertising, network, and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and limit advertising. The availability and selection of ad providers may be configured remotely.

Security Service

The app uses a security service to help protect the app and detect abuse. On supported physical devices, this service receives the app bundle identifier and an identifier derived from your device’s vendor identifier. It is not used to create an in-game profile.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your game progress. Information processed by advertising and security providers is shared only as needed to operate advertising, security, fraud prevention, and related app services described above.

Children

Eleventh Reset Paradox is a suspense and mystery game and is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to personalized advertising. Parents or guardians should review device privacy settings before use.

Your Choices

You can change tracking permission in your iOS Settings. You can reset local game progress from the in-game settings screen. Resetting progress does not change advertising permission managed by iOS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
